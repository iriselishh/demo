Our Mission

Our mission is simple — to offer immersive, responsible and unforgettable safari experiences that celebrate Botswana’s breathtaking wildlife, landscapes and culture while promoting sustainable tourism that supports local communities.

Mission & Values

  • Sustainability: We operate with minimal environmental impact.
  • Community: We support local guides, artisans and villages.
  • Conservation: We advocate for Botswana’s wildlife protection.
  • Authenticity: Every experience is genuine and meaningful.
The People Behind the Experience

Meet Our Guides

  • Alicia Moyo

    Night Safari & Astronomy Guide 6 years experience

    Oratile specialises in night safari experiences and unforgettable stargazing adventures. Growing up in the remote Kalahari, she brings the cosmos to life through San storytelling traditions and deep knowledge of Botswana's nocturnal wildlife.

  • Olebogeng Phuthego

    Cultural Experience Guide 7 years experience

    Olebogeng shares Botswana's living traditions through storytelling, village visits and cultural immersion. Raised in a traditional Batswana household, she brings warmth, authenticity and deep cultural knowledge to every experience she leads.

  • Cooper Ramosweu

    Lead Safari Guide 10 years experience

    Cooper brings extensive knowledge of Botswana's wildlife, bird species and safari tracking techniques. With over a decade guiding across Chobe, Moremi and Linyanti, his instinct for finding wildlife and reading the bush is unmatched.

Sustainability & Conservation

At NovaDrift Safarix, we believe responsible tourism and conservation go hand in hand. We minimize environmental impact, support local communities and actively contribute to the protection of Botswana’s wildlife and ecosystems.
