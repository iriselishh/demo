About NovaDrift Safarix

NovaDrift Safarix is a Botswana-based safari experience brand connecting visitors with the raw beauty of Africa's finest wilderness. From Chobe's elephant herds to Linyanti's starlit skies, we offer immersive experiences that go beyond the game drive - into the heart of Botswana's culture, landscapes and living heritage. Based in Gaborone, we operate across three of Botswana's most iconic destinations, guided by a team of passionate local experts.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I customize my safari experience?

Yes. We create personalized safari itineraries based on your interests, preferred activities, travel style and budget.

What should I pack for a safari?

We recommend lightweight neutral clothing, sunscreen, binoculars, comfortable walking shoes and a good camera. Avoid bright colours and strong perfumes around wildlife.

Which safari destinations do you offer?

We offer safari experiences across Chobe National Park, Moremi Game Reserve and Linyanti Wildlife Reserve.

Are your safari tours guided?

Yes. All our safari experiences are led by experienced local guides with deep wildlife expertise and safety training.

How do I make an enquiry?

You can reach us through the Contact page on our website or email us directly at info@novadriftsafarix.co.bw. We respond within 24 hours.

Are your safaris family friendly?

Yes. We offer experiences suitable for families, couples and solo travellers. Activities can be tailored to suit different age groups and fitness levels.
