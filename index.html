About NovaDrift Safarix

NovaDrift Safarix is a Botswana-based safari experience brand connecting travelers with the breathtaking beauty of Africa’s finest wilderness. From the elephant-rich plains of Chobe to the peaceful waterways of the Okavango Delta and the remote luxury of Linyanti, we create immersive safari journeys beyond ordinary travel experiences.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I customize my safari experience?

Yes. We create personalized safari itineraries based on your interests, preferred activities, travel style and budget.

What should I pack for a safari?

We recommend lightweight neutral clothing, sunscreen, binoculars, comfortable walking shoes and a good camera.

Which safari destinations do you offer?

We offer safari experiences across Chobe National Park, Moremi Game Reserve and Linyanti Wildlife Reserve.

Are your safari tours guided?

Yes. All our safari experiences are led by experienced local guides with deep wildlife expertise.

How do I make an enquiry?

You can contact us through the Contact page on our website or email us at info@novadriftsafarix.co.bw. We respond within 24 hours.

Are your safaris family friendly?

Yes. We offer experiences suitable for families, couples and solo travellers. Activities can be tailored to different age groups and fitness levels.
